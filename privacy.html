Privacy Policy

This policy covers the game Grapple Block: Backrooms Escape on iOS and Android, and this website. A German version is available: Datenschutz.

In short

  • Your progress, coins and settings stay on your device.
  • You can play without an account. Signing in (with a name and a password, with Apple or with Google) is optional and only serves to take your friends list to another device.
  • The game contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking services at this time.
  • Online play, the friends list, text chat and voice chat use services run by Unity. Your microphone stays shut until you press the talk key.

Playing alone

Your saved game, coins, settings and your list of blocked players are stored only on your own device. I do not run any servers that store player data. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Playing online

When the game starts, and when you open an online room or join one, the game uses services from Unity (Unity Software Inc., San Francisco, USA): an anonymous sign-in (Unity Authentication) and the connection between the players (Unity Multiplayer Services). The following is processed:

  • a random player ID, created at the first sign-in and stored on your device
  • your IP address and the room code
  • what the other players need to see: your chosen player name, your character, your position and your actions in the game

Your player name and player ID are sent to the other players in the same room. The ID is used so that a player you blocked stays blocked the next time you meet.

Signing in (optional)

Without signing in you play as a guest: the random player ID exists only on your device. If you sign in, Unity Authentication ties that player ID to the sign-in you chose, so that you can use it on another device. There are three ways:

  • A name and a password. Unity stores the account name you choose and your password in protected form. The name can be anything from 3 to 20 characters; if you use an e-mail address as the name, that address is stored as the name. I cannot see your password.
  • Sign in with Apple (iPhone and iPad). The game asks Apple neither for your name nor for your e-mail address. Apple gives the game a sign-in token with an identifier that is unique to you and this game; the token is passed to Unity, which stores the identifier.
  • Sign in with Google (Android). Google gives the game a sign-in token for your Google account; the token is passed to Unity, which stores your Google account's identifier. The token can also contain your name, your e-mail address and the address of your profile picture; the game does not read, show or store them.

Signing in moves only your online identity and your friends list. Coins, abilities and your saved game stay on the device. You can sign out at any time in the game. You can also delete your account yourself: in the game's menu, open the account window (the button that shows what you signed in with) and choose "Delete account". This removes the account, its sign-in and its friends list at Unity for good; the device then plays on as a new guest. If you no longer have the game, write to the e-mail address above and tell me your account name, or your player name with its tag, and I will delete the account for you.

Friends list

The friends list uses Unity's Friends service. It stores your player ID, your player name with a four-digit tag, your friends and open friend requests, and the players you blocked. While you play, your friends can see that you are online and, if your room has a free place, the room's code so they can join you. Invitations to a room are sent to the friend as a short message through the same service.

Text chat and quick messages

Text chat is switched off until you switch it on in the options. Typed messages then go to the players in the same room and pass through a word filter first. They are not stored permanently. Quick messages from the phrase wheel are sent only as a number.

Voice chat

In an online room, voice chat is on unless you switch it off in the options. It uses Vivox, a service from Unity, which processes your player name, player ID and IP address. Your own voice is processed only while your microphone is open, and is then sent live to the players in the same room who have voice chat on. I do not record or store conversations. When you block a player, Vivox receives that player's ID so that you no longer hear each other.

The game asks for access to the microphone for this. By default the microphone is open only while you hold the talk key or have tapped the microphone button. If you do not allow microphone access, you can still hear the others but are not heard. Without voice chat the microphone is not used. You can switch voice chat and the microphone off again at any time in the options, and mute or block single players.

Reporting a player

When you report a player in the game, the game prepares an e-mail in your own mail app. It holds the room, the names and IDs of both players, the reason you chose and the reported player's last chat lines. It is sent only if you send it. I use it to check the report and delete it once it is no longer needed for that.

Purchases

Purchases in the game are handled by the store you downloaded it from (Apple App Store or Google Play). I do not receive your payment details. The store tells the game only that a purchase was made, so the game can hand out what you bought.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, write to the address above and I will delete it.

Legal bases and recipients

For players in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies. The online features, signing in and the friends list are provided at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Using the microphone is voluntary: by allowing microphone access and opening the microphone you give your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time by switching the microphone or voice chat off (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Reports are processed to keep the game safe for everyone (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The recipient of the data named above is Unity, as the operator of the online, sign-in, friends and voice services. If you sign in with Apple or Google, that company (Apple Inc. or Google LLC, both USA) learns that you signed in to this game and processes the sign-in under its own privacy policy. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. Details are in Unity's privacy policy. I do not sell personal data and do not share it with anyone else.

This website

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising services and embeds no third-party content. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data: your IP address, the date and time, the file requested and details of your browser and operating system (server logs). The legal basis is the legitimate interest in delivering the site safely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

If you write me an e-mail, I use what you send to answer your request, and delete the message once it is no longer needed and no legal duty requires keeping it.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you is stored, and have it corrected or deleted. Under the GDPR you also have the right to restrict or object to the processing, the right to data portability, and the right to complain to a data protection authority. To use any of these, write to the e-mail address above. Most of your data is on your own device and is removed when you delete the game. Deleting the game does not delete an account you signed in with: delete the account in the game first ("Delete account" in the account window), or write to me to have it deleted.

Changes

If the game gains new features that process data, such as advertising, this policy will be updated before those features are released.
