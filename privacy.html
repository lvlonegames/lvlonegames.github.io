Privacy Policy

This policy covers the game Grapple Block: Backrooms Escape on iOS and Android, and this website. A German version is available: Datenschutz.

In short

  • Your settings stay on your device. Your coins, gems, abilities, skins, VIP status and play statistics are also kept at Unity under a random player ID, so that they cannot be tampered with and follow you to another device.
  • The public leaderboards show your player name, your time played and your number of escapes to all players.
  • You can play without an account. Signing in (with a name and a password, with Apple or with Google) is optional and serves to take your friends list and what you have earned to another device.
  • The game contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking services at this time.
  • Online play, the friends list, text chat and voice chat use services run by Unity. Your microphone stays shut until you press the talk key.

Playing alone

Your settings and your list of blocked players are stored only on your own device; deleting the game deletes them. What you have earned or bought in the game (coins, gems, ability levels, skins, VIP or Founder status, and the counters behind them, such as how many coins and ad rewards a day has already paid) is in addition kept at Unity (Unity Cloud Save), under the random player ID described below and without your name. The same goes for your play statistics (runs, deaths, time played, run times, where your coins came from), what you have found (rooms, endings, entities, comics) and your picks (the skin you wear, the abilities you equip), so that they follow you to another device. The game sends Unity what happened (for example: a run was finished, an ability was bought), the game's server code, which runs at Unity (Unity Cloud Code), works out the result and sends it back. This keeps these values from being changed by cheating tools. I do not run any servers of my own.

Leaderboards

The game has two public leaderboards: time played and number of escapes. When you finish or leave a run, the game's server code adds your time and escapes to them. Every player can see the lists, with your player name (without its four-digit tag), your time played and your escapes. The lists are kept at Unity (Unity Leaderboards). Deleting your account in the game also removes your entries.

Who is online (owner only)

While the game is open, it tells the game's server code every two minutes that you are playing: your player ID, your player name, whether you are in the menu, in a run on your own or in an online room, and which room. Only I, as the game's owner, can see this, in the game's admin page, to know how many people play and to help in rooms. Each player's latest entry is kept for two days and then deleted. It is not shown to other players.

Playing online

When the game starts, and when you open an online room or join one, the game uses services from Unity (Unity Software Inc., San Francisco, USA): an anonymous sign-in (Unity Authentication) and the connection between the players (Unity Multiplayer Services). The following is processed:

  • a random player ID, created at the first sign-in and stored on your device
  • your IP address and the room code
  • what the other players need to see: your chosen player name, your character, your position and your actions in the game

Your player name and player ID are sent to the other players in the same room. The ID is used so that a player you blocked stays blocked the next time you meet.

Signing in (optional)

Without signing in you play as a guest: the random player ID exists only on your device. If you sign in, Unity Authentication ties that player ID to the sign-in you chose, so that you can use it on another device. There are three ways:

  • A name and a password. Unity stores the account name you choose and your password in protected form. The name can be anything from 3 to 20 characters; if you use an e-mail address as the name, that address is stored as the name. I cannot see your password.
  • Sign in with Apple (iPhone and iPad). The game does not ask for your name. It asks for an e-mail address, and Apple lets you choose: share your address, or "Hide My Email", in which case Apple makes up a private relay address for this game. Apple gives the game a sign-in token with an identifier that is unique to you and this game, plus that address; the token is passed to Unity, which stores the identifier. The game does not show or store the address itself (see "One e-mail, one account" below).
  • Sign in with Google (Android, and iPhone once it is available there). Google gives the game a sign-in token for your Google account; the token is passed to Unity, which stores your Google account's identifier. The token can also contain your name, your e-mail address and the address of your profile picture; the game does not read, show or store your name or picture.

One e-mail, one account. So that signing in with Apple on one phone and with Google on another leads to the same account, the game sends the sign-in token to the game's server code. It checks that the token is genuine, made for this game and that the e-mail address in it is confirmed, and then keeps only a scrambled form of the address (a one-way hash), never the address itself, together with the player ID it belongs to. When a token with the same address comes in later, the game offers you that account (for an account with a password, it asks for the password once). An address hidden with Apple's "Hide My Email" is not used for this. The hash is kept privately at Unity (Unity Cloud Save) and is never shown to players.

Signing in moves your online identity, your friends list and what is kept under your player ID (coins, gems, abilities, skins, VIP status, play statistics) to the other device. Settings stay on each device. You can sign out at any time in the game. You can also delete your account yourself: in the game's menu, open the account window (the button that shows what you signed in with) and choose "Delete account". This removes the account, its sign-in and its friends list at Unity for good; the device then plays on as a new guest. If you no longer have the game, write to the e-mail address above and tell me your account name, or your player name with its tag, and I will delete the account for you. On request I also delete the coins, abilities and other values kept under your player ID.

Friends list

The friends list uses Unity's Friends service. It stores your player ID, your player name with a four-digit tag, your friends and open friend requests, and the players you blocked. While you play, your friends can see that you are online and, if your room has a free place, the room's code so they can join you. Invitations to a room are sent to the friend as a short message through the same service.

You can give a friend a skin as a gift. The gift (who gave which skin to whom) is recorded in both players' data kept at Unity, and the friend gets a short message about it through the same service.

Text chat and quick messages

Text chat is on unless you switch it off in the options. Typed messages go to the players in the same room and pass through a word filter first. They are not stored permanently. Quick messages from the phrase wheel are sent only as a number.

Voice chat

In an online room, voice chat is on unless you switch it off in the options. It uses Vivox, a service from Unity, which processes your player name, player ID and IP address. Your own voice is processed only while your microphone is open, and is then sent live to the players in the same room who have voice chat on. Voice chat works by distance: players near your character in the game hear you, players far away do not; for this, Vivox receives your character's position in the room. I do not record or store conversations. When you block a player, Vivox receives that player's ID so that you no longer hear each other.

The game asks for access to the microphone for this. By default the microphone is open only while you hold the talk key or have tapped the microphone button. If you do not allow microphone access, you can still hear the others but are not heard. Without voice chat the microphone is not used. You can switch voice chat and the microphone off again at any time in the options, and mute or block single players.

Reporting a player

When you report a player in the game, the game prepares an e-mail in your own mail app. It holds the room, the names and IDs of both players, the reason you chose and the reported player's last chat lines. It is sent only if you send it. I use it to check the report and delete it once it is no longer needed for that.

Purchases

Purchases in the game are handled by the store you downloaded it from (Apple App Store or Google Play). I do not receive your payment details. The store tells the game only that a purchase was made; the game's server code then asks the store whether the purchase is real and paid before it hands out what you bought.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, write to the address above and I will delete it.

Legal bases and recipients

For players in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies. The online features, signing in and the friends list are provided at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Using the microphone is voluntary: by allowing microphone access and opening the microphone you give your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time by switching the microphone or voice chat off (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Reports, the check against cheating, the e-mail hash that keeps one account per address, the leaderboards and the owner's view of who is online are processed to run the game fairly and safely for everyone (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The recipient of the data named above is Unity, as the operator of the online, sign-in, friends, voice, save, server code and leaderboard services. If you sign in with Apple or Google, that company (Apple Inc. or Google LLC, both USA) learns that you signed in to this game and processes the sign-in under its own privacy policy. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. Details are in Unity's privacy policy. I do not sell personal data and do not share it with anyone else.

This website

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising services and embeds no third-party content. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data: your IP address, the date and time, the file requested and details of your browser and operating system (server logs). The legal basis is the legitimate interest in delivering the site safely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

If you write me an e-mail, I use what you send to answer your request, and delete the message once it is no longer needed and no legal duty requires keeping it.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you is stored, and have it corrected or deleted. Under the GDPR you also have the right to restrict or object to the processing, the right to data portability, and the right to complain to a data protection authority. To use any of these, write to the e-mail address above. Your settings are on your own device and are removed when you delete the game.
