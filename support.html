Support

Something broken, stuck or strange in Grapple Block? Write to me and I will look into it.

Contact

levelonegames1@gmail.com

Or ask in the community: Grapple Block Discord

It helps if you include

  • Your device and its system version (for example: iPhone 13, iOS 18)
  • The game's version number
  • What you were doing and where (the room name is shown when you enter a room)
  • What happened, and what you expected instead
  • A screenshot or a short video, if you have one

Purchases

Refunds for purchases are handled by the store you bought from (Apple App Store or Google Play). If a purchase did not arrive in the game, write to the address above and include the receipt.
