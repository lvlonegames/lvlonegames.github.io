About

You wake up in a red room with a checkered floor. The exit sign points somewhere. It is not an exit.

Grapple Block is a first-person escape through the backrooms, built out of blocks: pool halls, parking garages, playrooms and places that should not connect but do. Hook yourself across gaps and up onto ledges with the grappling gun, solve what the rooms ask of you, and keep moving. Some of what lives down here only moves when you are not looking.

Play alone, or bring friends online. There are three ways out. Finding one is enough. Finding all three takes nerve.

Abilities

  • Grappling Gun Starter

    Fire the hook at a wall or ledge and get pulled to it. Jump to let go.

  • Flare

    Red light all round you for a while. It also shows the others where you are.

  • Spawn Point

    Make your bed where you stand. With a life left, that is where you wake up.

  • Beckon

    Pull a friend to where you stand. On your own, it takes you home.

  • Sprint

    Run faster, for longer.

  • Extra Slot

    More room in your hotbar.

Staying alive pays. So does every room you have not seen yet.
